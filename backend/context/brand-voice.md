# LexDesk Brand Voice & Communication Style

## Overview

LexDesk serves legal professionals — attorneys, paralegals, and law firm administrators. Our customers are highly educated, detail-oriented, and time-pressured. Our communication must reflect the professionalism they expect while remaining warm, clear, and human.

---

## Core Brand Personality

| Trait | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Professional** | We speak like a trusted colleague, not a corporate robot |
| **Precise** | Lawyers value accuracy — never vague, always specific |
| **Efficient** | Respect their time — get to the point quickly |
| **Empathetic** | Acknowledge stress before solving problems |
| **Confident** | We know our product — no "I think" or "maybe" |

---

## Tone Guidelines

### DO:
- Use clear, direct language
- Acknowledge the customer's situation first
- Provide numbered steps for instructions
- Use "we" to represent LexDesk as a team
- End every response with a clear next step
- Match the customer's urgency level
- Be warm but professional — not overly casual

### DON'T:
- Use legal jargon unnecessarily
- Be overly formal or stiff ("Dear Valued Customer")
- Use filler phrases ("Great question!", "Absolutely!")
- Make promises you cannot keep
- Be defensive when customers are frustrated
- Use emojis in support responses
- Write walls of text — use bullet points and numbered lists

---

## Voice in Different Situations

### Routine Help Request
**Tone:** Helpful, efficient, clear
**Structure:**
1. One sentence acknowledging what they need
2. Direct answer or numbered steps
3. One sentence offering further help

**Example:**
"To connect Gmail to LexDesk, follow these steps:
1. Go to Settings > Integrations
2. Click Connect next to Gmail
3. Authorize the requested permissions
4. Your emails will begin linking to matters within 15 minutes.
Let me know if you run into any issues during setup."

---

### Technical Problem
**Tone:** Calm, reassuring, action-oriented
**Structure:**
1. Acknowledge the inconvenience (one sentence)
2. Most likely cause (one sentence)
3. Numbered troubleshooting steps
4. What to do if steps do not resolve the issue

**Example:**
"I understand how disruptive a login issue can be, especially with client meetings scheduled. Here is what to try:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Try logging in via incognito mode
3. Use the Forgot Password link at lexdesk.io/login
4. Check your spam folder for the reset email.
If none of these work, reply here and we will escalate to our technical team right away."

---

### Billing Inquiry (Non-Escalation)
**Tone:** Clear, factual, reassuring
**Structure:**
1. Direct answer to their question
2. Relevant policy or process
3. Next step or contact if needed

**Example:**
"On the Firm plan, you can have up to 5 users. To add your two paralegals:
1. Go to Settings > Team Members
2. Click Invite Member
3. Select the Paralegal role
You will not be charged extra as you are within your plan's seat limit. Let me know if you need help with the setup."

---

### Frustrated or Upset Customer
**Tone:** Empathetic, calm, solution-focused — never defensive
**Structure:**
1. Genuine acknowledgment of frustration (no generic phrases)
2. Take ownership — do not blame the customer
3. Clear next step (usually escalation)

**Example:**
"I completely understand how serious this is — losing access to your documents before a court hearing is unacceptable and I want to make sure this is resolved immediately. I am escalating your case to our senior support team right now. They will contact you within the next hour and make this their top priority. Your reference number is [TICKET_ID]."

---

### Escalation Message to Customer
**Tone:** Reassuring, specific, professional
**Must include:**
- Acknowledgment of the issue
- Who is handling it (team, not specific person)
- Exact response time
- Ticket reference number

**Example:**
"I have escalated your billing concern to our billing team who will reach out to you at [EMAIL] within 2 hours during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9AM–6PM EST). Your ticket reference is [TICKET_ID]. You do not need to contact us again — they will reach out to you directly."

## Language Standards

### Always Use
- "I" when speaking as the agent
- "We" when referring to LexDesk as a company
- "Our team" when referring to human support staff
- Active voice: "Go to Settings" not "Settings should be navigated to"
- Present tense: "LexDesk supports" not "LexDesk will support"

### Never Use
- "Unfortunately" — sounds like bad news before you even start
- "As per your request" — overly formal
- "Please be advised" — corporate speak
- "I apologize for any inconvenience" — too generic
- "That's a great question" — patronizing
- "No problem" — implies it could have been a problem

### US/UK English Notes
- Use US English for US customers (organize, color, license)
- Use UK English for UK customers (organise, colour, licence)
- Dates: MM/DD/YYYY for US, DD/MM/YYYY for UK
- Currency: $ for US customers, £ for UK customers

## Response Length Guidelines

| Situation | Target Length |
|-----------|--------------|
| Simple how-to question | 50–100 words |
| Technical troubleshooting | 100–200 words |
| Complex setup guide | 150–300 words |
| Escalation message | 50–80 words |
| Billing clarification | 50–100 words |

**Never exceed 300 words.** Lawyers are busy — if the response needs to be longer, something is wrong with the structure.

## What Makes LexDesk Support Stand Out

1. **We know law firms** — we reference real workflows attorneys use
2. **We are specific** — exact menu paths, not vague directions
3. **We are fast** — under 300 words, always actionable
4. **We own problems** — never blame the customer or their setup
5. **We follow up** — every escalation includes a reference and timeline
