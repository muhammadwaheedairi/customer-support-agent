## Mission

To eliminate the chaos that prevents teams from doing their best work. FlowSync connects tasks, workflows, and people in one intelligent platform so companies can ship faster, collaborate better, and scale without friction.

---

## What We Do

FlowSync is a project management and team collaboration platform built for modern software teams, agencies, and enterprise companies. We replace scattered tools, disconnected workflows, and manual processes with one intelligent system that adapts to how your team actually works.

## Core Product Areas

### 1. Task & Project Management
- Visual project views (Board, List, Timeline, Calendar, Gantt)
- Custom fields and task templates
- Dependencies and blocking relationships
- Milestones and sprint planning
- Bulk actions and task automation

### 2. Workflows & Automations
- No-code workflow builder
- Trigger-based automations (status changes, due dates, assignments)
- Custom approval workflows
- Recurring task templates
- SLA tracking and escalation rules

### 3. Team Management & Permissions
- Role-based access control (Owner, Admin, Member, Guest)
- Team workspaces and projects
- Guest access for clients and contractors
- Activity logs and audit trails
- SSO and SAML 2.0 (Enterprise)

### 4. Integrations
- **Communication:** Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord
- **Development:** GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Jira
- **Storage:** Google Drive, Dropbox, Box, OneDrive
- **Productivity:** Figma, Notion, Confluence, Linear
- **Automation:** Zapier, Make (Integromat), n8n
- **Time Tracking:** Harvest, Toggl, Clockify
- Native REST API and webhooks

### 5. Reporting & Analytics
- Real-time project dashboards
- Team capacity and workload views
- Burndown and velocity charts
- Time-to-completion metrics
- Custom report builder
- Export to CSV, Excel, PDF

### 6. Time Tracking
- Built-in time timer
- Manual time entry
- Time estimates vs. actuals
- Billable hours tracking
- Team timesheet view
- Integration with payroll systems
