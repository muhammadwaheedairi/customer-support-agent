## Mission

To eliminate the administrative burden that prevents lawyers from doing what they do best — practicing law. LexDesk automates client intake, document management, billing, and case tracking so law firms can focus on winning cases, not managing paperwork.

---

## What We Do

LexDesk is an AI-powered law firm management platform built specifically for small to mid-size law firms (2–50 attorneys) in the US and UK. We replace the chaos of spreadsheets, email threads, and legacy software with one intelligent platform.

---

## Core Product Areas

### 1. Client Intake Automation
- AI-powered intake forms that qualify leads automatically
- Conflict of interest checks
- Automated follow-up sequences
- E-signature for retainer agreements

### 2. Case Management
- Matter tracking with deadlines and task assignments
- Document storage with version control
- Court date calendar with automated reminders
- Client portal for real-time case updates

### 3. Time & Billing
- One-click time tracking
- Automated invoice generation
- LEDES billing format support
- Online payment via credit card, ACH, and wire transfer

### 4. Document Automation
- Template library (500+ legal document templates)
- Auto-fill from client/matter data
- Clause library for custom drafting
- PDF generation and e-signature

### 5. Client Communication
- Secure client messaging portal
- AI-powered FAQ responses
- Automated status update emails
- SMS notifications for deadlines
